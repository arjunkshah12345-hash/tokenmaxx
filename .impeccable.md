## Design Context

### Users
AI coding power users who treat token burn as a sport. They open TokenMaxx to check rank, connect more sources, and flex volume.

### Brand Personality
Dry · Competitive · Precise. Short copy, no corporate fluff.

### Aesthetic Direction
Clean minimalist paper UI: cool off-white canvas (#FAFAF9), near-black ink, hairline borders, generous whitespace. Visual signature is **dither-kit** (ordered-dither charts, avatars, gradient washes) — not neon scoreboard chrome. Avoid purple gradients, cream+terracotta editorial, and dense card grids. Brand “TOKENMAXX” is the hero signal; dither fills are the dominant visual plane.

### Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast; respect prefers-reduced-motion; ranks never rely on color alone.
