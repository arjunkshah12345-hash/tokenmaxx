Get on the board

Install SuperCompress. When your account starts burning tokens, a row appears on the board with tokens passed and tokens compressed. No handle claim, no link step.

Two steps

  1. Install SuperCompress

    One command. It detects your coding agents and wires them up. Your SuperCompress account is your board identity.

    npx -y supercompress-proxy setup
    npx -y supercompress-proxy setup

    Works with

  2. Keep using it

    TOKENMAXX syncs SuperCompress accounts every few minutes. New accounts with usage show up on their own. No handle to claim, no link command.

    Board: tokenmaxx.supercompress.dev/board

How it counts

Totals only. From SuperCompress.

  • Tokens passed: tokens that went into SuperCompress from your coding agents.
  • Tokens compressed: tokens SuperCompress removed (passed minus kept).
  • Each SuperCompress account maps to one board handle, derived from the account. No separate claim step.
  • Past days count on that UTC day. Live growth between syncs counts in the hour it is seen, so hourly stays honest.
  • Hourly / daily / weekly / monthly windows are UTC and reset on the clock.
